Sunset Cover Studio turns your photos into neon sunset posters in your browser. Start with six sample photos or add your own, then position and rotate each crop, edit the panel captions, and add a title and subtitle.

Choose from eight layouts: Mosaic · 6 panels, Hero + strip, Three panels, 2×2 grid, Hero right, Centre title, Comic · 6 panels, and Single hero. Tune one of five color grades: Vice Sunset, Neon Night, Golden Hour, Comic Ink, or Noir Heat. Eight toggles control halftone, grain, speed lines, location tags, the badge/seal, the volume chip, the ribbon, and the quote strip.

Open `app/index.html` in desktop Chrome to work offline and export full-resolution PNGs in landscape (1920 × 1080), portrait (1080 × 1350), or square (1080 × 1080). Export one size or all three.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
